## 1. The sun, rebuilt to match your reference

The old sun was a flat disc with a soft gradient. Yours has layered concentric
glow rings and long tapered rays. Now:

- **Four concentric wash rings** stepping down in opacity — the layered halo.
- **Fifteen tapered rays** at deliberately uneven angles (evenly spaced ones read
  as clip-art), each a narrow triangle so it comes to a point. Every third ray is
  longer and heavier.
- Ray length and ring spread scale with the sun's halo, so one component covers a
  low hazy dawn and a high blazing midday.

I rendered it and found the midday sun was nearly invisible: its colour was
`#fffbe0` — near-white on a near-white sky. Midday is now `#ffd83d` on a light blue
sky, morning `#ffdc4a`, dawn `#ffb98a`. Ray weight went from 2–3px to 5–7.5px.

## 2. Absent days showed no LOP

An absent day usually has **no attendance row at all**, and the calendar returned
`lopDays: record?.lopDays || 0` — with no record, that is `0`. Absent days now
derive 1.0 LOP (half day 0.5) from the resolved status. It only disappears when
leave is applied *and* approved, because the day then resolves to `LEAVE` rather
than `ABSENT`. Each day also carries `lopSource` so the UI can show why.

## 3. Route not found: POST /api/dashboard/ai-widget

The endpoint genuinely did not exist — `api/aiWidgets.js` called it and silently
fell back to a local generator. It exists now, and Gemini returns a widget **spec**
(type/title/size/tint/config) which the controller allow-lists field by field, so a
bad model response can never inject anything into the dashboard.

Both new routes verified against a live server: `401` (mounted, needs auth), not `404`.

## 4. Corner chat assistant

Bottom-right bubble on every page. Ask "Is Aindrila present today?", "What was her
in time?", "How many present today?".

**Read-only by design.** It answers from the database through
`POST /api/dashboard/assistant`, and every lookup is filtered through your own
employee scope — it cannot surface someone you could not already open in the
directory. Salary questions additionally require payroll read access, and draft
payroll is never quoted.

Anything that *writes* stays on the AI Assistant page, where there is a permission
check, a dry-run preview and an audit trail. A floating bubble is the wrong place
to approve leave or change a salary.

## 5. Skeleton loading

Was a background-position shimmer on a grey bar. Now a real sweep of light travels
across each placeholder on a cubic-bezier easing, children stagger by 90ms so a card
reveals as a wave rather than pulsing as one block, and the placeholders sit on
proper card surfaces. Disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 6. Ambient scenery on every page

The workspace now carries a soft, time-of-day tinted sky behind all content, with a
faint skyline silhouette along the bottom — the same motif as home. It is fixed and
non-interactive, so it reads as depth rather than competing with the data.

Driven by `document.body.dataset.period`, set from the **same clock** the home
skyline uses, so the two can never disagree about the time of day. Six palettes
(`--sky-hi/-lo/-sun/-glow/-far`) cross-fade over 1.2s.

**Text contrast was handled explicitly**, since this was the risk: cards and tables
sit on `rgba(255,255,255,.93)` with a backdrop blur, heroes on `.80`, headings pinned
to `--ink` and body copy to `--ink-2`. At night body copy darkens further so contrast
never drops. I rendered midday and evening and checked legibility in both.
